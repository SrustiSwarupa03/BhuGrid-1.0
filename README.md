# BhuGrid 1.0

**The single secure vault for every sacred legacy across Odisha.**

A GIS-based Religious Institution and Asset Management Platform designed to support the geographic visualization, secure management, monitoring and preservation of religious institutions and their associated properties and assets across Odisha.

BhuGrid connects institutional geographic information with property and asset records, inspection workflows and administrative review, supporting structured information management and accountable institutional oversight.

## Prototype Scope and Disclaimer

> **BhuGrid 1.0 is a controlled DEMO prototype.** Its geographic inventory contains provisional and unverified GIS features, while its three Supabase DEMO institutions and illustrative property records are fictional. Public GIS points and AI-generated suggestions do not constitute official property verification, proof of ownership, legal boundary determination or administrative decisions.

The platform is intended to support authorized institutional stakeholders, including Local Bodies, Trustees / Institutional Management and Government Agencies, alongside Inspector and Administrator workflows.

Access to institutional information is intended to be restricted according to administrator-approved permissions and assigned institutional responsibilities. Production readiness, authoritative data verification and comprehensive security validation remain subject to further development and testing.

**Documentation scope:** This repository contains public project documentation. It does not publish or license the private application source code, backend implementation, credentials, private database information or deployment configuration.
